The product in 60 seconds

Each Mandate = an AI worker wrapped in an employment contract. MandateType is the class (charter · faculties · settlement · gym); MandateInstance is the object (one per business, the moat — its private heap grows as it works); MandateRun is the frame (does the work, then dies at settlement).

The kernel is dumb and deterministic — it routes triggers, runs gated syscalls, journals every effect. The Foundry is smart and gated — runs the candidate through a sandbox swarm, grades it, and (only) on real graded wins promotes a new version.

Why it's defensible

  • Heap per business — private moat that compounds with every settle.
  • Gym + compiler — mandates that improve themselves (rare in this category).
  • No credential in user space — import-lint-enforced. Premium for any business that doesn't trust generic agents.
  • Verifiable, journaled settlement — show a customer a real receipt, not a black-box log.
  • Creator mandate — your cost of adding new products drops from sprints to minutes.

How to add more mandates

① Use the Creator (drafts, soon chat)

Today: the Creator drafts a candidate MandateType. The conversational UI is G10 (next session).

② Add a MandateType in code (canonical)

Mirror build_lead_finder_type() as build_<name>_type() in packages/mandate/src/agentx_mandate/library/. ~80 lines. Register in api/app.py:91. Add tests, run gate.

③ Wire a new syscall adapter

Add an Adapter in packages/syscall/src/agentx_syscall/adapters.py. Set rung & risk class. Credential via ConfigVault (pod never sees it). Money = API-only, gated.

④ Run a swarm to iterate a candidate

POST /commands/run-swarm drives the candidate through the kernel in sim, promptfoo grades, PromotionGate bars synthetic-only.

  • "OS for business" is a category, not a product — sell the lead-finder, not the OS.
  • The gym is post-PMF defensibility, not the first sale.

⚠ The one risk worth naming: velocity

The architecture was the right thing to nail first — and you did, honestly, very well. But the hardest thing in agent-platform businesses is not the kernel, it's the rate of real-world feedback. You have 1 settle, you need ~100. Drinking your own champagne (you as the customer, every week, for 12 weeks) is the cheapest path to settle #100, the most honest product feedback loop, and the demo that closes your first paid pilot. Build the 2nd mandate to feed the same loop.
