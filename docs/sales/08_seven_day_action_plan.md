# 08 — Your 7-Day Action Plan (Week 1)

> End state by Sunday: **you've sent real outreach to 30+ hand-picked prospects, run the
> follow-ups, and booked at least one conversation.** One metric. No overwhelm.

**The ONE metric you track:** **# of outreach touches sent.** Not replies. Not deals. *Sent.*
(Why: it's the only thing you control, and it's the cure for fear — file 06.) Target for Week 1:
**≥ 45 touches** (first sends + follow-ups).

**Volume guardrail (don't skip — it protects your domain):** while your cold domain warms
up, keep **manual sends ≤ 10–15/day** from a warmed inbox. This week is about *quality reps
+ setup*, not blasting. Real scale comes after the 4–6 week warmup (file 03).

---

## Day 0 (Sunday night, 30 min) — Setup so Monday has no excuses
- [ ] Confirm the 3 drafted emails are ready (SDO CPA, Cucciniello, Maxim Liberty).
- [ ] Start **Wise Business** signup (verification runs in background — file 07).
- [ ] Buy a **separate sending domain** (e.g., a `.co`/`.io` variant of your brand) for cold
      email later. Set up **SPF + DKIM + DMARC** and turn on inbox **warmup** today so it's
      ready in ~4–6 weeks. *(This week you send from a warmed personal/Gmail-business inbox,
      low volume.)*
- [ ] Make a dead-simple tracker (a sheet): columns = Name · Company · Channel · Day sent ·
      Follow-up dates · Reply? · Notes.

## Day 1 (Monday) — SEND. (target: 8 touches)
- [ ] **9:15 — Send the 3 drafted emails.** Read each aloud once, hit send. *(Hardest rep done.)*
- [ ] Send **5 warm DMs** (file 03 #1) to anyone who might know a CPA/bookkeeper/agency owner.
- [ ] Use the lead-finder to generate **this week's narrow target list (~30)** for cold email.
- [ ] Log everything. **Close the laptop.** Today's win = 8 sent. ✅

## Day 2 (Tuesday) — Cold batch #1 (target: 10 touches)
- [ ] Hand-pick **10** from your list. Personalize the file-03 cold template (one true detail each).
- [ ] Send the 10. Connect with the same 10 on **LinkedIn** (note from file 03).
- [ ] Log. Done for the day.

## Day 3 (Wednesday) — Cold batch #2 + first follow-ups (target: ~12)
- [ ] Send the next **10** cold emails (+ LinkedIn connects).
- [ ] **Follow-up #1** to Monday's batch: "Bumping this — still happy to send the free 5."
- [ ] If anyone replied: send them the **free 5** (file 02 handoff). 🎉

## Day 4 (Thursday) — Follow-ups + LinkedIn (target: ~10)
- [ ] **Follow-up** to Tuesday's batch.
- [ ] LinkedIn "want me to send 5?" message to anyone who connected.
- [ ] Handle any replies with file 04. If a call gets offered, schedule it for Day 6/7 and
      **keep file 05 open during it.**

## Day 5 (Friday) — The "give one of the 5" touch (target: ~10)
- [ ] **Day-7-style value bump** to your earliest non-repliers: send **one** real sample
      prospect + its cited line — "want the other 4?" (This lifts replies hard.)
- [ ] Send any remaining cold emails to hit ~30 first-touches for the week.
- [ ] Book/confirm any call for the weekend or early next week.

## Day 6–7 (Weekend) — First conversation + review
- [ ] **Run your first call** if booked — read file 05 verbatim. Goal: a "yes" to the free 5.
- [ ] **Close-out touch** to anyone gone cold: "I'll stop here — reply 'send' anytime and the 5
      are yours."
- [ ] **Weekly review (15 min):**
  - Touches sent this week: ___ (target ≥45)
  - Replies: ___ · Free-5 samples sent: ___ · Calls booked: ___
  - What got the most replies? Do more of that next week.

---

## What "success" looks like by Sunday
You will likely have **1–3 replies** and **maybe 1 conversation** from ~30 cold + a handful of
warm touches. **That is a normal, good Week 1** at 2026 reply rates — and every warm reply
is worth 10× a cold send because your free sample converts them. If you got **zero replies**,
you did not fail: you completed ~45 reps, learned your domain/targeting, and you adjust the
*list* and *subject line* next week (30% list, 30% copy, 50% follow-up).

---

## Week 2 preview (so you know it compounds)
- Double the cold volume (still ≤15/day until the warmed domain is ready).
- Add 5–7 touch sequences to *everyone* from Week 1 (50% of replies live in follow-up).
- Turn your first happy reply into a **testimonial** → use it to start charging the retainer.
- First paid batch → first retainer. You're now *doing*, not reading.

> **Reality check (your critical friend):** the trap isn't Week 1 effort — it's Week 2 silence
> making you quit. Most people stop right before the follow-ups (where the replies are). Put
> the follow-up days in your calendar **now** as non-negotiable appointments. Showing up on
> Day 10 is the whole game.
