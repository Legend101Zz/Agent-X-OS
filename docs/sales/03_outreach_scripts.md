# 03 — Outreach Scripts (Cold Email · DM · Call)

> Built on **$100M Leads**, Section III — the **Core Four** (#1 Warm Outreach, #2 Post Free
> Content, #3 Cold Outreach, #4 Run Paid Ads). Every script is short, specific, and ends with
> **one** CTA. Read them out loud — they're written to be spoken/sent verbatim.

---

## Read this before you send anything (it will save your domain)

The 2026 data is blunt, so let's be real (sources in the HTML report):
- Average cold-email reply rate is **~3.43%** (down from 8.5% in 2019). Generic blasting is dead.
- BUT **narrow lists (<200 people) get ~2× the reply rate and ~4.4× the positive replies** of
  big lists. **Email + LinkedIn = ~1.9× positive replies; Email + Call = ~2.5×.**
- **30/30/50 rule:** 30% of success is your copy, 30% is list quality, **50% is follow-up.**
- Gmail/Yahoo 2026: set up **SPF + DKIM + DMARC**, keep spam complaints **under 0.30%**,
  use a **separate domain** (never your main one), and **warm it 4–6 weeks** (5–10/day) before
  volume. (Setup steps are in file 08.)

**Translation for you:** your lead-finder is built for the thing that *works* — tiny, hyper-
targeted, evidence-based lists — not the thing that fails. Play to that. Start with **warm +
DM**, layer cold email once the domain is warm, and **always multi-touch.**

## CORE FOUR #1 — WARM OUTREACH (start here; lowest fear, highest reply)

People who already know you / are one step removed. Goal: not to pitch — to *offer the free
sample.* Hormozi's ACA flow (Acknowledge → Compare → Ask) underlies these.

**DM / email to anyone you've ever interacted with (founders, ex-colleagues, communities):**
> "Hey [Name] — I built something that finds a service business its 5 best-fit potential
> clients (real, named, with proof they fit) and writes the first outreach email for them. I'm
> giving the first sample free to a few people to get feedback. Do you know any **accounting,
> bookkeeping, or agency owner** in the US/UK who'd want 5 free? Even a name helps."

## CORE FOUR #3 — COLD EMAIL (the workhorse; keep it 75 words, one CTA)

Research rule for accountants: **lead with their operational problem, not your credentials.**
"75 words with a specific pain and a specific ask beats a 250-word capabilities pitch."

## CORE FOUR (cross-channel) — LinkedIn / DM

LinkedIn first because as an unknown overseas sender it builds trust email can't, and
**Email + LinkedIn = ~1.9× replies.**

**Connection note (≤300 chars):**
> "Hi [Name] — I help [CPA/bookkeeping] firms get 5 free, verified, best-fit prospects (with
> the first email pre-written). Not selling anything here — happy to just send you the 5 to
> get your read on the quality."

**After they accept (wait a day):**
> "Thanks for connecting! Genuinely — want me to send 5 real, named businesses that fit
> your ideal client this week, free? Each comes with proof from their own site + a drafted
> first email. Just say the word and I'll put them together."

## COLD CALL — opener, voicemail, and the 5–7 touch sequence

You said calls scare you. **You do not start here.** You start with email/DM. Calls come
*after* a few have replied warmly. When you're ready, read this verbatim.

**Opener (permission-based, disarming):**
> "Hi [Name], this is Mrigesh — you don't know me, so I'll be quick and you can hang up if
> it's useless. *[pause]* I build a system that finds firms like yours 5 real, named best-fit
> prospects with the first email pre-written — and I'd like to send you 5 free. Can I grab the
> best email to send them to?"

That's it. The call's only goal is **permission to send the free 5** — not to close. Low stakes.

**Voicemail (≤15 sec):**
> "Hi [Name], Mrigesh here. I've got 5 real, named best-fit prospects for [Company] ready to
> send you free — with the first email already written. I'll email you the details; subject line
> 'free 5.' Talk soon."

**The 5–7 touch sequence** (50% of results live here — most people quit after one):

| Day | Channel | Action |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Email | Cold email (template above) |
| 1 | LinkedIn | Send connection note |
| 3 | Email | Reply to your own thread: "Bumping this — still happy to send the free 5. Worth a look?" |
| 5 | LinkedIn | If connected: the "want me to send 5?" message |
| 7 | Email | Value bump: "Here's *one* of the 5 to show what I mean: [single real prospect + the cited line]. Want the other 4?" ← give a taste |
| 10 | Call/VM | Opener or voicemail above (only if comfortable) |
| 14 | Email | Polite close-out: "I'll stop here so I'm not a pest — if finding good-fit clients ever gets back on your plate, just reply 'send' and the 5 are yours." |

> **Why the Day-7 "give one of the five" works:** it converts your lead magnet into a live
> demo mid-sequence. It's the salty pretzel — one taste, and they want the rest.

## Reality check (your critical friend)

- **Most won't reply. That is normal and not about you.** At ~3% reply, 30 *good* targets ≈ 1
  conversation. That's why narrow + multi-touch + the free sample (which lifts reply rates
  hard) matters. Volume of *reps*, not perfection — see file 06.
- **Never mass-send from your real domain, and never fake an email address.** Your whole
  brand is "real and verified." One spammy blast can torch your domain for months.
- **One CTA per message. Always.** "Want the free 5?" Nothing else.
