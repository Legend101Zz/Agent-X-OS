---

## Why a lead magnet (the story that should give you confidence)

In *$100M Leads*, Hormozi's webinar **failed** — "Three days, $450, 80 leads, and **0 sales**
later… I shut it all down." Then he posted a simple **free case-study video** ("FREE Case
Study: How we added 213 members and $112,000 in revenue to a small gym in San Diego")
and woke up to *"strangers have booked my calendar solid for the next week."* His lesson:

> **"They didn't want my webinar. But they did want my case study… getting leads works
> when you give people something they want."**

**Your free 5-prospect sample IS that case study.** It's not a brochure — it's *visible proof*
of the exact result they want, with their own market's names on it.

---

## What a lead magnet is (the definition to honor)

> A lead magnet is **"a complete solution to a narrow problem"** — lower-cost or free — that,
> once solved, **reveals another problem solved by your core offer.** — *$100M Leads*

- Salty-pretzels logic: solve "I'm hungry" for free → reveal "I'm thirsty" → sell the drink.
- **"A person who pays with their time now is more likely to pay with their money later."**
- **"If they think your lead magnet is worth their time, they'll think your core offer is worth
  their money."**
- **"Be a (legal) drug dealer — give the strongest hit first."** → Your free 5 must be your
  *best* work, not a watered-down teaser.

## Your lead magnet, run through Hormozi's 7 steps

**Step 1 — The narrow problem + who:**
*Problem:* "I don't know which specific, real businesses are a good fit for me right now, and I
hate hunting for them." *Who:* owner of a US/UK CPA, bookkeeping, or B2B service firm.

**Step 2 — How to solve it:** Your sample is *all three* Hormozi lead-magnet types at once —
which is the strongest kind ("the dressing room" that does all three):
1. **Reveal Their Problem** — "Here are 5 ideal clients in your market you are *not* reaching
   right now." (Shows revenue left on the table — a problem that gets worse the longer they wait.)
2. **Sample / Trial** — it's a real, working sample of the paid service.
3. **One Step of a Multi-Step Process** — finding is step 1; *contacting + handling replies +
   closing* are the steps your paid offer covers.

**Step 3 — How to deliver it** (Hormozi's 4 mechanisms — you hit three):
- **Software** (the mandate generates it), **Information** (the cited evidence + fit reasoning),
  **Services** (you hand-verify + draft the outreach). Deliver as a clean one-page PDF/email +
  optionally a 2-minute Loom walking through it.

**Step 4 — Name it** (test names; Ogilvy: "when you've written your headline you've spent
80¢ of your dollar"). Candidates:
- "Your 5 Best-Fit Clients This Week — Found, Verified, and Pre-Drafted (Free Sample)"
- "5 [accounting/bookkeeping] firms you should be talking to — with the first email written"

**Step 5 — Easy to consume:** one page. Each prospect = name, one-line why-they-fit, the
cited website quote, the contact path, the drafted email. Skim-able in 90 seconds.

**Step 6 — Make it darn good:** these are your **real, verified** finds — SDO CPA LLC
(Duncanville TX, 0.88), Anthony J. Cucciniello CPA (Morristown NJ, 0.85), Maxim Liberty
(bookkeeping-services.com, 0.92). Real beats generic every time.

**Step 7 — Make it easy to say "I want more":** end with one line + one CTA (below).

## The free → paid handoff (script this — don't wing it)

The sample's job is to earn the right to ONE ask. Put this at the bottom of the sample, and
say it on the call:

> "These 5 took my system about a day to find, verify, and draft. I can do this **every week**
> — 20 fit-scored prospects with the first email written, so you just approve and send. The
> first 5 you're holding are free. If they're useful, the next batch of 20 is **[$300 one-time /
> $750 a month]**, and you only continue if it's clearly worth it. Want me to line up this
> week's 20?"

**The reveal that closes it** (research-backed — see file 04): your sample silently answers the
#1 objection to "AI leads" — that they're junk, recycled, never-heard-of-you contacts.
Yours come *with proof from the prospect's own site and a real contact path*. Let the
quality make the argument; you just point at it.

---

## Reality check (your critical friend)

- **Don't give away 50 for free to "prove yourself."** Five is enough to show quality and
  keeps your scarcity real. Over-giving trains them to expect free.
- **The free sample must look hand-finished, not machine-dumped.** The whole edge is "this
  isn't a scraped list." If it reads like a scraped list, you've lost. Verify each one yourself.
- **One CTA only.** Don't end with "let me know your thoughts." End with "Want this week's 20?"
