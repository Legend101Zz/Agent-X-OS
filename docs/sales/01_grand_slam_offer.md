# 01 — Your Grand Slam Offer

> Built on **$100M Offers** (Hormozi, 2021). Frameworks cited inline. Nothing here is a
> fabricated quote — where I quote Alex, it's a phrase verified in the book.

---

## The one idea this whole file rests on

You are **not** selling "AI leads." You are selling the thing the research says buyers
actually can't get from junk-list vendors: **named, verified prospects with proof from
their own website, a real contact path, and the first message already written.** That is a
*category of one*. Price it like one.

> **Commodity = price-driven purchase (race to the bottom). Differentiated = value-driven
> purchase (sell in a category of one).** — *$100M Offers*, Ch. 3 (The Commodity Problem)

Hormozi's own flagship example in the book is *literally a lead-gen business*. He shows how
a commoditized "lead generation services" offer ($1,000 down + $1,000/mo retainer, 0.5:1
return — you **lose** money getting customers) becomes a Grand Slam Offer of **pay-for-
performance**, producing **2.5× response × 2.5× close × 4× price = 22.4× cash collected**
(*$100M Offers*, Ch. 2). Read that chapter twice. It's your business.

---

## The Value Equation (the spine of every offer below)

> **Value = (Dream Outcome × Perceived Likelihood of Achievement) ÷ (Time Delay × Effort & Sacrifice)**
> — *$100M Offers*, Ch. 6

Two drivers you **raise**, two you **drive toward zero**. The four questions in your buyer's head:

| Driver | Buyer's question | How Agent-X answers it |
|---|---|---|
| **Dream Outcome** (↑) | "What will I get?" | New client conversations on my calendar without me hunting |
| **Perceived Likelihood** (↑) | "How do I know it'll work?" | They see 5 *real, named* prospects with cited evidence **before paying** |
| **Time Delay** (↓) | "How long?" | First 5 in **72 hours** |
| **Effort & Sacrifice** (↓) | "What's expected of me?" | Almost nothing — I find, verify, and draft; you approve & send |

Hormozi's lesson: beginners over-invest in the top (big claims are easy and lazy). **"The
best companies focus on the bottom of the equation — making things immediate, seamless,
and effortless"** (Apple, Amazon, Netflix). Your tool already crushes the bottom: it removes
the prospecting *and* the writing. Lead with that.

## OFFER #1 (LEAD WITH THIS) — "The Booked-Calendar Guarantee"

**For:** US/UK service firms that sell to other businesses — CPA & bookkeeping firms,
agencies, B2B consultants. (Your real found leads are exactly this: SDO CPA, Cucciniello
CPA, Maxim Liberty.)

**Dream outcome, in their words:**
> "I want a steady trickle of *good-fit* prospects already teed up — without buying junk
> lists, hiring an SDR, or learning to cold-email myself."

**The core offer (say it out loud):**
> "Every week I hand you **20 named, verified businesses that match your ideal client** —
> each with (1) a fit score, (2) proof pulled from *their own website* of why they fit, (3) a
> real reachable contact path (a working form or named decision-maker, never a guessed
> email), and (4) a personalized first-touch message **written for you, ready to approve and
> send.** You do nothing but say 'yes, send' or tweak a line."

**Risk reversal / guarantee** (*$100M Offers*, Ch. 15 — Guarantees):
> "Your **first 5 prospects are free**, delivered in 72 hours, so you judge the quality before
> you pay a cent. And on every paid batch: if you don't agree that at least **15 of the 20**
> are genuine fits, **you don't pay** — I redo the batch free."
This is a *conditional / 'better-than-money-back'* guarantee — it reverses their risk AND
yours (you only carry risk on quality you control).

**Urgency / scarcity** (Ch. 12–13 — honest, not fake):
> "I onboard **3 new firms a month** because I verify every prospect by hand — when those
> are gone, there's a waitlist." (True: your time is the real constraint. Don't invent fake
> timers; the capacity limit is real and that's *better*.)

**Bonuses** (Ch. 14 — Bonuses; name and value each):
- **Bonus 1 — The drafted outreach** (already in core, but *name* it): "Done-for-you first
  email + 2 follow-ups per prospect." *(Worth more than the leads — it's the part you hate.)*
- **Bonus 2 — "Reply Playbook":** the exact replies to send when a prospect responds (from
  file `04_objection_handling.md`).
- **Bonus 3 — Monthly "who's underserved" mini-report** from the discovery mandate: one
  niche worth chasing next quarter.

**Price + why:**
- **Free pilot:** 5 prospects, 72 hrs. (This is your lead magnet — see file 02.)
- **Starter:** **$300–$500 for a one-time batch of 20** verified prospects + drafted outreach.
- **Retainer (the goal):** **$750–$1,500/month** for 20/week (≈80/mo) + drafted outreach +
  bonuses. *Why this works:* if even **one** new client is worth $3k–$30k/yr to a CPA firm,
  your fee is a rounding error. You're charging a *fraction of what they make* — exactly
  Hormozi's premium-pricing logic (Ch. 5). **"There is no strategic benefit to being the
  second cheapest, but there is for being the most expensive."** — Dan Kennedy, quoted in
  *$100M Offers*, Ch. 5.

> **Reality check (your critical friend):** Don't price this at $50 to feel safe. A $50 price
> *signals junk* and attracts the worst clients (Hormozi, Ch. 5: low price → low perceived
> value → worst clients → no margin). But also: you're new and unproven, so the **free 5 →
> paid 20 → retainer** ladder is what earns the right to the retainer price. Land 2–3 firms
> on the starter price first; raise prices once you have a testimonial.

## OFFER #2 — "Pay-For-Performance" (the 22.4× offer, for the brave)

This is Hormozi's actual book example, adapted. Use it once you trust your delivery.

> "I'll find and draft outreach to qualified prospects **for free**. You only pay me **when a
> prospect you contacted books a call** — $X per booked call (or $Y per closed client). I
> guarantee **at least 5 booked calls in the first 60 days or you owe nothing.**"

**Why it's a 'feel-stupid-saying-no' offer:** their downside is zero. You carry the risk. That
*is* the Grand Slam move — but only take it when your lead quality + drafted outreach are
genuinely converting, or you'll work for free. Start with Offer #1; graduate to this.

## OFFER #3 — "Clean-Books / GST-Ready Prep" (your India wedge, keep warm)

For Indian SMBs + their Chartered Accountants (books-prep mandate, in build).

> "I prep your books and GST so your CA just *reviews* instead of doing the grunt work —

**Why keep this in your back pocket:** local market = easier trust, language, payment,
timezone (the cross-border friction we cover in file 07 disappears). But the mandate is still
in build, so it's *Offer #3*, not your lead. **"Pick one. No one can serve two masters... Don't
make me niche slap you."** — *$100M Offers*, Ch. 4. **Lead with Offer #1. Commit.**

## How these were "trimmed & stacked" (Ch. 9–10)

Hormozi's offer-build = list every problem → list a solution to each → **trim** the
low-value/high-cost items → **stack** the high-value/low-cost ones. For you:
- **Kept (high value, low cost to you):** named prospects, cited evidence, contact path,
  drafted email + follow-ups, reply playbook. (All produced by the mandate — near-zero
  marginal cost. This is your unfair advantage: the value equation's bottom is already ~0.)
- **Trimmed:** "we'll also run your ads / manage your CRM / do your branding." No. That's
  scope creep that re-commoditizes you and burns your time. Stay narrow.
